R8 -- DUTY RESOLUTION: THE REACHABLE BAND AGAINST HoldCycleTime
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A duty-cycled auger cannot ask for an arbitrarily small duty. The harness
asserts `cycle_time*ratio >= 1 and cycle_time*(1-ratio) >= 1`
(controller_matrix.py:169), so the smallest duty it can express is
1/HoldCycleTime. THAT BOUND IS A HARNESS ARTEFACT, NOT A PHYSICAL ONE:
`_auger_toggle_tick`'s own docstring says production evaluates the toggle
at ~20 Hz onto an auger that integrates fuel continuously, so the shipped
resolution is far finer than 1 s. The real floor is whatever the auger
mechanism and pellet delivery impose, and NOBODY HAS MEASURED IT. The
table below is therefore the band this SIMULATION can express, and it is
an upper bound on the restriction a real grill suffers.

The binding minimum duty is max(u_min setting, 1/HoldCycleTime); the rows
below set u_min to the resolution floor, so they isolate resolution.

More fan RAISES the duty a low setpoint needs, so at the bottom of the
range the fan is what makes a setpoint expressible. That is the opposite
of a brake: it is operating range.

VALIDATION AGAINST A KNOWN REAL DATUM. The user reports this physical
grill is controllable from 180 F to 600 F, and the logged cook
reached 520 F. A plant model that cannot express that range would be
miscalibrated, the way H=140 was before it was corrected to 420.
  180 F needs duty 0.0479 (fan 1.0) -- expressible from 25 s cycle upward
  600 F needs duty 0.3329 (fan 1.0) -- against u_max 0.90
  verdict: MAKGrillSim REPRODUCES the user's stated 180-600 F range.
