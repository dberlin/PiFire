MAKGrillSim IS NOT A REPLAY. The real 450 F MAK cook is a fixed log; a
controller that acts differently gets nothing different back from a CSV, so
real data cannot be run closed-loop at all. MAKGrillSim carries that log's
identified parameters (C_c=3115.9, H=958.8, 100 s deadtime), which is the
closest a closed-loop measurement can get to that grill.

WHICH GUARD ACTUALLY DECIDED, AND OVER WHAT DENOMINATOR. A12's identifiability
floor could only ever be reached by the F3 refits: `_IDENTIFIABILITY_FLOOR` and
`update_mpc.identifiability` DO NOT EXIST at 282ee65c9309, so the other half of
the refits above never evaluated it. Over the 18 refits that could, it never
bound -- a 3 h hold from a COLD start is not a flat record, and its ramp
determines the model. F5's refusals never reach that test either: `evaluate`
short-circuits on the PROMOTION_BOUNDS check for h_amb first. Those bounds are
byte-identical at both revisions -- h_amb (1e-4, 1e3) -- so what separates the
arms here is the fitted value, not a change to the gate.
