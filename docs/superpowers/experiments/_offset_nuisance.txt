SECTION 2 -- what the fourth parameter costs in identifiability
============================================================================================================
s_min is the smallest singular value of d(prediction)/d(parameter) over the arm's free set,
per sample. Adding a column can only lower it: the minimum is taken over a larger space.
Arm B's fourth column is exactly ones, so its NORM is exactly 1 and arm B's s_min is bounded
above by 1.0 on every record that exists -- see the arm_svals docstring. Arm C's log column
is about T_amb=20 times the same physical move, so its per-degree reading is also printed and
is the one comparable to arm B.

  arm               median       p10       min   below 0.50    of   newly refused
  A_shipped         2.8224    0.0000    0.0000           36   136               0
  B_offset          0.2206    0.0000    0.0000          136   136             100
  C_free_amb (log)    0.0000    0.0000    0.0000          117   136              81
  C_free_amb (per C)    0.0054    0.0000    0.0000          136   136             100

'below 0.50' counts records the floor would refuse under that arm; 'newly refused' counts those
that clear it today under arm A and would stop clearing it. Population is every fitted record,
including the flat synthetic ones the floor exists to refuse -- they are already below it under
arm A, so they cannot be newly refused and do not inflate that column.

Could the floor simply be RE-DERIVED under a four-parameter arm? That is the obvious rescue, so
it is measured rather than dismissed. A12 set 0.50 between the records that determine nothing and
the records that do, and the room it had is the ratio between the two classes. UNINFORM here is
A12's own structural class, constant-free: the flat synthetic cooks and each plant's steady_hold,
which contain exactly one transient-free operating point by construction. The real cook is the
record the feature exists to learn from, so any floor must sit below it and above that class.
